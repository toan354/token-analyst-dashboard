# Research Report Reader Guide

## How to Read This Report

This research report synthesizes on-chain behavioral data into a structured narrative format. It is designed for **research analysts** and **due diligence professionals** who need to understand observable on-chain patterns without actionable recommendations.

### Report Flow

1. **Start with the Executive Summary** — Get the high-level takeaways in 5-7 bullet points
2. **Check L1 Gatekeeper Status** — Understand the overall health signal (PASS/WARNING/FAIL)
3. **Review L2 Structural Context** — See how market/supply structure frames the L1 outcomes
4. **Examine L3 Behavioral Evidence** — Detailed flow patterns, whale activity, and destination analysis
5. **Read L4 Research Narrative** — Synthesized hypotheses with explicit source citations
6. **Note Data Confidence & Limitations** — Understand what data was available and its quality

### Key Concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **L1 Gatekeeper** | Binary health checks (PASS/WARNING/FAIL) |
| **L2 Structural** | Risk modules with defined thresholds |
| **L3 Behavioral** | Descriptive flow patterns (NO verdicts) |
| **L4 Narrative** | Interpretive synthesis (NO recommendations) |

### Traceability

Every claim in the L4 Research Narrative section is tagged with its source:
- `[L1]` — From Gatekeeper status
- `[L2]` — From structural context modules
- `[L3]` — From behavioral observations

This allows you to verify any statement by tracing it back to its origin module.
